Terms of service

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Calendar Alarms is a personal Windows alarm utility. By connecting it to Google Calendar, you authorize read-only Calendar event access; the app uses that access only for timed events in your primary calendar and local alarm scheduling. You can disconnect access through your Google Account settings and remove local data using the documented uninstall command.

The app needs Windows to be running and an audible output device to sound an alarm. It cannot wake a computer that is shut down or asleep. After a restart or resume, an alarm that is no more than 15 minutes overdue may sound; older alarms are marked missed. Internet or Google API failures can delay discovery of calendar changes, although the last successfully synchronized schedule remains available locally. Alarm delivery can also be affected by Windows clock changes, muted sound, hardware, or operating system behavior. Do not rely on CalendarAlarms as the sole alert for critical commitments.

Calendar Alarms does not change Google Calendar events. Your use of Google Calendar remains subject to Google's terms.
