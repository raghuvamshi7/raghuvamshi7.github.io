CalendarAlarms

CalendarAlarms is a personal Windows system tray app that rings an audible alarm when a timed event starts in your primary Google Calendar.

It requests read-only access to calendar events. It reads event IDs and start times to maintain a local alarm schedule, checks for changes every 60 seconds, and shows times in India Standard Time. It ignores all-day events and does not read event titles, descriptions, or locations.

Alarms ring locally until you dismiss them. The app does not create, change, or delete Google Calendar events.
